Sanatana Knowledge Ecosystem

Decolonizing global knowledge through Vedic philosophy, Dharmic governance, ethical systems design, and civilizational research.

Overview

The Sanatana Knowledge Ecosystem is a multidisciplinary digital knowledge initiative exploring how ancient Indian wisdom traditions can inform modern governance, education, ethics, public systems, and societal transformation.

This repository combines:

Vedic philosophy
Dharmic ethics
Decolonial frameworks
Governance research
Cultural intelligence
Systems architecture
Ethical institutional design

The project presents Sanatana thought not as a relic of the past, but as a living knowledge system capable of contributing to contemporary global challenges.

Mission

To build an open civilizational knowledge platform that:

Preserves Indigenous Knowledge Systems
Reframes Bharat-centric intellectual traditions
Bridges ancient wisdom with modern institutions
Encourages ethical governance models
Promotes philosophical pluralism and cultural literacy
